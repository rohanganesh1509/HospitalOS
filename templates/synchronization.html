THEORY
Binary Mutex Locks (Operating Theatre & Scanner)

Guarantees mutual exclusion: only one patient process may enter the critical section at any instant. If a second process calls P(), it is blocked to WAITING and appended to the primitive's wait queue.

Counting Semaphores (ICU Beds)

Maintains an integer counter initialized to 3 units. Up to 3 processes can enter concurrently. When the counter drops to 0, subsequent processes are blocked until an existing occupant issues a V() signal.

Wait Queue & Wakeup Semantics

When a resource is released via release_lock(), HospitalOS signals the head of the wait queue, transitioning that process from WAITING → READY, ready to be dispatched by the CPU scheduler.
